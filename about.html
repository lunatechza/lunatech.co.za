Our Story

South African base, global delivery

Lunatech was established in 2014 in Jeffreys Bay, South Africa. Since then, we have evolved from a highly focused local software workshop into an enterprise-facing engineering powerhouse.

Although we operate from a beautiful coastal community, our reach is entirely global. We collaborate with international fintech platforms, sophisticated logistics networks, and enterprise partners to build reliable, high-throughput digital systems.

We avoid generic hype. Instead, our team takes direct, end-to-end responsibility for the architecture, scaling, security, and ongoing operations of the intellectual property we construct.

Our Core Beliefs

Operating principles

Build the right thing

We spend time thoroughly understanding real operational constraints before deciding on architecture, ensuring we don't build unnecessary complexity.

Own the technical outcome

Our engineering team takes full accountability from strategy and code structure all the way through continuous hosting and production monitoring.

Automate repeatable work

We programmatically eliminate manual, error-prone tasks. If an action is done more than twice, we write a workflow to handle it.

Keep systems understandable

Clean code, modular styling, and structured API documentation mean your team (and ours) can easily scale the codebase for years.

Protect trust and data

By avoiding unsafe integrations and enforcing zero-trust data access policies, we keep client financial, trade, and private data secure.

Improve continuously

Technology changes, and so do we. We dedicate active R&D hours to benchmarking new languages, models, and security tooling weekly.
