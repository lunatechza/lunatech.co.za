CASE STUDY · FINTECH & BLOCKCHAIN

Engineering the infrastructure behind a South African digital asset exchange.

ChainEX is a South African digital asset exchange that enables clients to trade, hold and manage supported crypto assets using ZAR markets.

Lunatech operates as a core strategic engineering partner to ChainEX, taking long-term technical responsibility for critical platform infrastructure.

Role Core Strategic Engineering
Status Operational in Production
SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

The challenge

Operating a digital asset exchange requires more than a trading interface. The underlying platform must coordinate orders, balances, deposits, withdrawals, wallet activity, external integrations and transactional records reliably.

These systems must remain traceable and maintainable while handling continuously changing market and regulatory requirements.

RESPONSIBILITIES

Lunatech’s role

Trading execution & exchange backend

High-performance order matching, balances, and market execution engines built for stability.

Transactional ledger & reconciliation

Immutable ledger accounting and automated reconciliation systems across liquid digital asset markets.

Wallet & blockchain automation

Secure wallet infrastructure, hot/cold custody automation, and deposit/withdrawal event processing.

API gateway & third-party integrations

Scalable REST/WebSocket API endpoints and third-party payment/banking service integrations.

Platform monitoring & ops

Continuous 24/7 infrastructure observability, automated alert pipelines, and production maintenance.

Ledger & transaction audit tooling

Custom analytical and transaction audit tools built for financial integrity and compliance reporting.

ENGINEERING STACK

Technology

TypeScript PHP MySQL Docker Redis Event-driven architecture API integrations Blockchain infrastructure
PARTNERSHIP APPROACH

Long-term platform ownership

ChainEX demonstrates Lunatech’s approach to complex software: understand the operational risk, engineer the critical paths carefully and remain accountable after the initial launch.

Our involvement extends beyond project delivery into ongoing platform engineering, infrastructure improvements, integrations and operational support.
